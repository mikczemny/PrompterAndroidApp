Prompter is designed to work locally on your Android device. We do not operate
an account system, advertising platform, analytics service or user-content
server for the app.

## Information processed on your device

- **Microphone audio.** While tracking is active, Prompter uses microphone audio
  to follow your position in a script. The same local stream can be saved as a
  WAV recording. Audio is not uploaded to us or to a speech-recognition service.
- **Camera video.** When you enable the selfie camera, Prompter displays a local
  preview. While recording, it can create a silent MP4 on your device. Video is
  not uploaded to us.
- **Scripts and imported documents.** Text you type, paste, save or import is
  processed locally. Imported files are accessed only after you select them
  through Android's system file picker.
- **App preferences.** Prompter stores settings needed for features such as the
  recording destination selected through Android's folder picker.

## Network use

Prompter uses the internet only to download the Vosk language model you select
from Alpha Cephei. After download, speech recognition works offline. Prompter
does not send your microphone audio, camera video, scripts or imported document
text with this request.

## Storage and sharing

Recordings are temporary until you choose Save. You may save them in the app's
device folder or a folder you select. You can delete saved recordings in the
app. Opening a video in another app grants that app temporary read access to the
selected file; the receiving app's privacy practices then apply.

Saved scripts remain in the app's private storage until you delete them or
uninstall the app. Android backup is disabled for Prompter.

## Data collection, sharing and retention

We do not collect or share personal data through Prompter because user content
is not transmitted to us. Retention is controlled by you on your device. An
uninstall removes app-private data; recordings saved in a user-selected shared
folder may remain until you delete them.

## Children

Prompter is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children.
It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Prompter uses Android runtime permissions, HTTPS model downloads, scoped file
access and local processing. No software can guarantee absolute security; keep
your device protected and choose recording folders appropriate for your content.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version and date
will be published at the same URL.
